---
title: "Maple chia pudding"
description: "Simple, healthy chia pudding with a hint of maple syrup"
created: 2026-06-14
---

This pudding is healthy, requires no cooking, and tastes like dessert.

- 4 tbsp chia seeds
- 1 c whole milk
- 1 tsp maple syrup
- 0.25 tsp ground cinnamon
- Pinch of salt

1. Whisk all ingredients in a mixing bowl.
2. Let sit at room temperature for at least 15m. Whisk again.
3. Let sit at room temperature for at least another 15m. Whisk for a third
   time.
4. Refrigerate overnight. When serving, thin with additional milk if necessary.
   Top with fruits, nuts, honey, and/or additional maple syrup.
